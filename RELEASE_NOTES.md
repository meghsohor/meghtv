## What's new

- **New TV menu.** The menu is laid out like the phone's: one row of tabs (Info, Refresh, Search, Favourites, Categories) with the list below it, and it's wider, so names fit.
- **Remote keys.** With the menu closed, Left/Right bring up the player controls and move between them, Pause and Stop included, and Up/Down open the menu. Back always closes the menu; Left goes back up a level, from a list to its category, then to the tab.
- **The menu remembers where you were.** It opens on the row you left it on, or on the channel that's playing, and keeps the list where it was.
- **New look for the lists.** Channels sit on rounded rows, and ones that didn't play are faint, so they're easy to spot. On TV only the focused channel shows its star; favourites always do.
- **Channel name always shown on TV** while a channel is on.
- **Clearer buttons.** All popup buttons look alike, and the selected one is filled, so it's easy to see which one OK presses.
- **On first launch the menu stays open** until you pick a channel.

## Install

Download **MeghTV-v1.4.0.apk** below and open it on your device. It updates MeghTV 1.0.0 or newer and keeps your favourites. You need Android 6.0 or newer. On a TV, an app such as Downloader can fetch and install the file.
