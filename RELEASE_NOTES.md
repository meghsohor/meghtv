## What's new

- **Stop.** A Stop button next to Play/Pause on a phone, and at the end of the bottom row on a TV (the remote's Stop key works too), ends the stream so nothing keeps downloading, and clears the picture instead of leaving it frozen. Play starts the channel again at live. Picking the same channel again, or another channel, plays it too.

## Install

Download **MeghTV-v1.3.1.apk** below and open it on your device. It updates MeghTV 1.0.0 or newer and keeps your favourites. You need Android 6.0 or newer. On a TV, an app such as Downloader can fetch and install the file.
